FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about Smart Subscription Advisor

Is Smart Subscription Advisor free to use?

Yes, it is 100% free to get started. You can track all your active subscriptions, receive advance renewal alerts, and view analytics without providing any credit card information.

How does the AI advisor detect savings opportunities?

Our deterministic rule engine analyzes your usage frequency, days until renewal, category overlaps (such as multiple concurrent streaming apps), and calculates exact rupee savings for your portfolio.

Is my financial data kept safe and private?

Yes. We use JWT-secured authentication, encrypted sessions, and isolated user databases. We never sell your personal subscription data to third parties.

Can I export my subscription portfolio to CSV?

Yes. You can export your full portfolio anytime in one click to CSV for personal budgeting or spreadsheets.
